Confirmation of Death

Hamas officials confirmed Mr. al-Haddad's death, but did not provide further details about the circumstances surrounding the Israeli strike.

The Israeli military has not commented on the incident, but has been conducting operations in Gaza in recent weeks.

Impact on Hamas

The group's military wing, the Izz ad-Din al-Qassam Brigades, has been responsible for numerous rocket attacks on Israel in recent years.
