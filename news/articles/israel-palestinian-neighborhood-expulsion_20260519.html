Activists say Israel tries to expel a whole Palestinian neighborhood in East Jerusalem

Israeli authorities are issuing demolition orders to Palestinians in East Jerusalem at an increased rate since the start of the war with Iran, according to activists and residents.

Activists say Israel tries to expel a whole Palestinian neighborhood in East Jerusalem

Background

The recent escalation in demolition orders has sparked concerns among Palestinians and activists, who claim that Israel is attempting to expel a whole Palestinian neighborhood in East Jerusalem. The neighborhood, which has been home to Palestinian families for generations, is now facing an unprecedented wave of demolition orders, leaving many residents at risk of displacement.

The Israeli government has long been accused of pursuing a policy of creeping annexation in East Jerusalem, with the aim of altering the city's demographic balance in favor of Jewish residents. The increased rate of demolition orders has been seen as a key component of this strategy, with many Palestinians facing the threat of displacement and homelessness.

According to data from the Israeli human rights group B'Tselem, the number of demolition orders issued in East Jerusalem has increased significantly since the start of the war with Iran. The group has documented numerous cases of Palestinian families receiving demolition orders, often with little or no warning, and with no alternative housing options provided.

Key details

Activists and residents have reported that the demolition orders are being issued at an alarming rate, with many families receiving notices that their homes are to be demolished within a matter of days or weeks. The orders are often based on claims that the homes were built without the necessary permits, although many Palestinians argue that it is impossible to obtain such permits due to discriminatory zoning laws.

Key facts:

  • Over 100 demolition orders have been issued in East Jerusalem since the start of the war with Iran
  • Many Palestinian families are at risk of displacement and homelessness
  • The Israeli government has been accused of pursuing a policy of creeping annexation in East Jerusalem
  • Discriminatory zoning laws make it impossible for many Palestinians to obtain building permits

The increased rate of demolition orders has also been accompanied by a rise in settler violence and harassment, with many Palestinians reporting incidents of intimidation and abuse. The situation has been further complicated by the lack of international attention and action, with many governments failing to condemn Israel's actions in East Jerusalem.

Impact

The demolition orders and displacement of Palestinian families are having a significant impact on the community, with many residents facing the loss of their homes and livelihoods. The situation is also exacerbating existing social and economic problems, including poverty and unemployment.

The increased tension and violence in East Jerusalem are also having a broader impact on the region, with many fearing that the situation could escalate into further conflict.

Conclusion

In conclusion, the increased rate of demolition orders in East Jerusalem is a serious concern that requires immediate attention and action. The international community must condemn Israel's actions and work to protect the rights of Palestinian residents, including their right to housing and self-determination.
