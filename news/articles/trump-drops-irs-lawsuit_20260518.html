Trump drops IRS lawsuit, paving the way for a settlement

Intro

Former President Donald Trump has dropped a lawsuit against the Internal Revenue Service (IRS) and the Treasury Department, a move that could pave the way for a potential settlement. The lawsuit, filed in January, sought $10 billion in damages over the leak of Trump's tax returns years ago.

The lawsuit was filed in response to the leak of Trump's tax returns, which were published by major news outlets in 2016 and 2017. The leak revealed that Trump had paid relatively little in taxes over the years, thanks to large deductions and business losses. The lawsuit alleged that the leak was the result of a conspiracy between the IRS, the Treasury Department, and other government agencies to harm Trump's reputation and undermine his presidency. The lawsuit named several current and former government officials as defendants, including the commissioner of the IRS and the secretary of the Treasury.

Trump's refusal to release his tax returns was seen as a departure from the practice of previous presidential candidates, who had typically made their tax returns public.

The lawsuit sought $10 billion in damages, which Trump claimed was the value of the harm caused to his reputation and business interests by the leak. The lawsuit also sought an injunction to prevent further leaks of Trump's tax returns and other confidential information.

However, the lawsuit faced significant legal hurdles, including the fact that the leak of Trump's tax returns was likely protected by the First Amendment.
